Case Study

Mapping the funding transition for local government

VDB turned fragmented data on the European Structural Investment Fund into a regional evidence base and decision-making tool using mapping to steer local government conversations around the UK Shared Prosperity Fund.

ClientLocal Government Association
SectorLocal government and public policy
CapabilitiesResearch; mapping, data analysis and visualisation
Period2021
Context

An evidence base for a major funding transition

As European Structural and Investment Funds were replaced by the UK Shared Prosperity Fund, local councils needed to understand what investment had reached their areas and what could be lost in the transition.

VDB built a structured match-funding database, analysed historic investment, consulted councils and produced a report with regional case studies. A user-facing, map-based dashboard made the findings usable beyond the report, showing how funding varied by place and programme so local and national decision-makers could compare the emerging replacement landscape.

The work gave the LGA and its member councils a shared evidence base for policy discussions, consultation responses and engagement with central government.
