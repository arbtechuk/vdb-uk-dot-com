Case Study

Building a fundable model for remote-sensing nature finance

VDB worked with Treeconomy to design a multi-stakeholder project that linked LiDAR and GIS-enabled monitoring with tokenisation of carbon-linked financing.

ClientTreeconomy Ltd
SectorNatural capital, agriculture and climate technology
CapabilitiesProject design with complex mapping technologies; consortium brokerage; fundraising
Period2022
Context

Connecting credible measurement with trusted carbon markets

Forest carbon markets depend on credible measurement and traceability. Treeconomy and Urstamm wanted to connect satellite and drone data, machine learning and blockchain technology so carbon could be measured at source, authenticated and traded with greater confidence.

VDB worked with Treeconomy and Urstamm to turn that technically ambitious proposition into a focused 12-month international R&D programme. The project we designed incorporated multiple novel sensor-based mapping technologies, a smart inventory-management system and a blockchain-enabled carbon-trading platform.
