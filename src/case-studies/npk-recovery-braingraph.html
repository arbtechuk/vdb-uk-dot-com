Case Study

Using space data to design smarter waste infrastructure

VDB designed a proof of concept digital twin for waste networks in the UK using Earth Observation and GIS.

Venture and partnerNPK Recovery / Braingraph
SectorSpace assets, utilities and digital modelling
CapabilitiesProduct design; funding strategy; partnership building; digital product definition
Period2025
Context

Connecting infrastructure decisions through spatial data

Waste infrastructure decisions are often made from disconnected datasets. The WAISTE proof of concept was a spatial decision-making model that connected waste demand, environmental spill risk, agricultural nutrient demand and existing infrastructure.

VDB developed the investment and delivery case with NPK Recovery and Braingraph. The proposal combined satellite Earth Observation, public and partner datasets, rules-based or machine-learning analysis, and cloud GIS tools. Its scoped outputs were a model architecture, a visual prototype and a plan for a full build.
