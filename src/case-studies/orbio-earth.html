Case Study

From a winning ESA proposal to commercial methane intelligence

VDB developed Orbio.earth's successful ESA Business Applications proposal and managed the documentation that carried a 12-month satellite-analytics demonstration through delivery and final review.

ClientOrbio Earth GmbH
SectorClimate technology and Earth Observation
CapabilitiesFunding strategy; programme documentation; research and innovation
Period2023–2024
Context

Turning emissions data into decision-useful intelligence

Methane emissions data is fragmented, inconsistent and difficult to compare across assets. Orbio.earth set out to turn satellite imagery, ground data and machine learning into decision-useful emissions intelligence for operators, data businesses and investors.

VDB developed the winning ESA Business Applications proposal, then worked with them through the project to develop the business plan and research and development plans and deliver project reporting to remain compliant with the funder.
